## Per-condition verdicts

### `forward_vx02` — **PASS** (AGENTS.md-mandated condition 1)
Clear alternating bipedal gait. Trunk vertical in all 20 frames, no forward
pitch and no tipping. Rows 1–2 lead with one leg, rows 3–4 with the other;
the swing foot is visibly off the floor while the stance foot is planted flat.
Deep-knee duck posture throughout — the nominal pose, matching the reference
motion, not a crouch-crawl. No dither. Aggregate for this condition:
gait-valid, 0.000 % falls, duty 68.06 / 65.38 %.

### `turn_wz03` — **PASS** (AGENTS.md-mandated condition 2)
Body yaw advances gradually across the strip, consistent with 0.3 rad/s over
the 2.5 s window, while the trunk stays upright. The feet are **picked up and
replaced** — a stepping rotation, not a pivot-scrape. No dragging foot.
Aggregate: gait-valid, 0.000 % falls, duty 72.55 / 69.84 %.

### `turn_wz05` — **PASS**
The same stepping rotation at the faster yaw rate; the robot turns from
side-on through front-facing to angled inside the window. Trunk upright,
alternating swing with clearance, no scrape. Aggregate: gait-valid,
0.000 % falls, duty 71.49 / 69.19 %.

### `press_wrench` — **PASS, and it shows a behaviour v5d did not have**
Under the sustained press the robot walks upright, and at **≈ 7 s the trunk
pitches steeply forward** — visibly close to the 60° tilt termination — with
the head low and one leg extended behind. **It catches the stumble and returns
to upright within about a second**, then keeps stepping for the remaining 13 s.
The final 4 s show it on its feet, trunk vertical.

Compare the m2657 audit of v5d on the same clip type: *"walks upright for
roughly the first 12 s under the sustained press, then topples and stays down
for the remainder — it does not self-right."* **This is a recovery v5d did not
show**, and it is what the contact curriculum was added to buy.

**Read no rate off this clip.** It is 2 envs and one draw; the measured rate is
**70.365 %** over 3,840 episodes. The clip establishes the mechanism, not its
frequency — and it is a favourable draw.

### `obstacle_graze` — **PASS**
The robot walks alongside the slab, contacts it, and continues upright past it,
trunk vertical throughout. Aggregate: **7.318 %** falls over 3,840 episodes, so
most episodes survive and this clip is representative rather than lucky — but
it is still one draw of 2 envs.

## What this audit does NOT establish

- **It is not a comparison with v5d.** Those clips were rendered on a 3.657 kg
  or 2.657 kg plant, at a 1.78x higher torque ceiling, with no latency model,
  and — for the wrench — under a press whose yaw torque was silently discarded
  (CFG-1). See `rebuild_results.md` §1.
- **Get-up from a completed fall was not tested.** The press clip shows recovery
  from a deep *lean*, not from a robot lying on the floor. Phase S must still
  assume that a completed fall ends the run until a human intervenes.
